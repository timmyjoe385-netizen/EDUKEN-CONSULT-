# EDUKEN CBT — next question batch (staging only)

## Scope
Create 50 new original questions each for:
- Christian Religious Studies (canonical subject: Christian Religious Studies)
- Commerce
- Principles of Accounts (student label: Financial Accounting)

Total planned: 150 questions. Existing published questions must remain unchanged.

## Difficulty allocation per subject
- Easy: 8 (16%)
- Moderate: 25 (50%)
- Challenging: 17 (34%)

This is the closest whole-question allocation to the agreed 15% / 50% / 35% target for a 50-question batch.

## Content requirements
- Questions must be original EDUKEN practice items, not copied past questions.
- Four distinct options (A–D), one defensible answer, and a concise explanation.
- Use syllabus-based coverage and avoid repeating concepts already tested in the first 50-question batch.
- Moderate and challenging items should emphasise interpretation, application, calculations, multi-step reasoning and plausible distractors.
- Difficulty must come from the thinking required, not confusing wording, obscure facts or trick phrasing.
- For CRS, anchor interpretation to the relevant biblical passage and avoid doctrinal ambiguity.
- For Commerce, use realistic business and trade scenarios; avoid changeable legal/tax rates unless the question supplies the rate.
- For Principles of Accounts, show enough figures and assumptions for calculations to be independently checked.

## Quality and release gates
1. Structural CSV validation: required fields, four distinct options, valid answer key, explanation, allowed difficulty and original-content provenance.
2. Subject-specific syllabus and topic coverage check.
3. Independent answer-key and calculation verification.
4. Exact and near-duplicate screening against the live bank and within this batch.
5. Import to isolated test project as draft-only; verify counts and sample student attempts.
6. Keep production untouched until the user explicitly approves the reviewed batch for import/publication.
