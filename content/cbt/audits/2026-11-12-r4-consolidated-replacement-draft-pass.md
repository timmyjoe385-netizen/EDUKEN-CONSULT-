### DUP-112 — Biology (held; not retained)
**Held source:** batch 33, row 14  
**Draft ID:** R4-ADD-2026-11-12-DUP-112  
**Topic:** Plant physiology / Phototropism

**Question:** When a young shoot bends towards a source of light, the response is known as

A. Geotropism  
B. Phototropism  
C. Hydrotropism

### DUP-147 — Chemistry
**Held source:** batch 34, row 18  
**Draft ID:** R4-ADD-2026-11-12-DUP-147  
**Topic:** Practical chemistry / Titration apparatus

**Question:** During an acid-base titration, which apparatus delivers a measured, variable volume of solution into a flask?

A. Pipette  
B. Burette  
C. Evaporating dish  
D. Filter funnel

**Correct option:** B

**Explanation:** A burette delivers accurately measured variable volumes of solution, including dropwise additions near the titration end point.

**Internal QA:** The apparatus has a clear, specific function. A pipette is used to transfer a fixed measured volume; the other options do not perform controlled titrant delivery.

**Full-bank screen:** Highest same-subject character-trigram Dice score: 0.342 across 200 Chemistry questions; no score at or above 0.42. The closest items concern indicators and salt preparation, not the function of a burette.

### DUP-226 — Biology
**Held source:** batch 37, row 14  
**Draft ID:** R4-ADD-2026-11-12-DUP-226  
**Topic:** Genetics / Crossing over

**Question:** The exchange of segments between non-sister chromatids of homologous chromosomes during meiosis is called

A. Independent assortment  
B. Mutation  
C. Crossing over  
D. Fertilisation

**Correct option:** C

**Explanation:** Crossing over occurs during prophase I of meiosis and exchanges corresponding chromosome segments between non-sister chromatids, contributing to genetic variation.

**Internal QA:** Tests a specific source of genetic variation rather than simply asking which division produces haploid gametes. The distractors represent distinct genetic or reproductive processes.

**Full-bank screen:** Highest same-subject character-trigram Dice score: 0.333 across 200 Biology questions; no score at or above 0.42. No close lexical match identified. This remains a draft because the bank contains related meiosis material and the owner should check whether the detail is appropriate for the intended syllabus level.
