## Draft 1 — Biology

- **Held topic:** Evolution / Natural selection
- **Draft topic:** Genetics and evolution / Gene flow
- **Stem:** The movement of alleles from one population to another as individuals migrate and reproduce is called:
- **Options:**
  - A. Gene flow
  - B. Genetic drift
  - C. Mutation
  - D. Artificial selection

- **Explanation:** Gene flow is the transfer of alleles between populations when individuals migrate and reproduce. It can introduce new alleles into a population and change its genetic variation.

- **Held topic:** Nutrition / Enzymes
