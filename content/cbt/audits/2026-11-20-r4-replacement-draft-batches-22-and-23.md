- **Proposed topic:** Figures of speech / Onomatopoeia
- **Difficulty:** Medium

**Question:** The word “buzz” is an example of which figure of speech?

A. Onomatopoeia  
B. Alliteration  
C. Assonance  
D. Hyperbole

**Correct option:** A

**Explanation:** Onomatopoeia is the use of a word whose sound imitates or suggests the sound it describes. “Buzz” imitates a continuous humming sound.

**Internal content checks**

- **Answer logic:** A is the only defensible answer. Alliteration repeats initial consonant sounds; assonance repeats vowel sounds; hyperbole is deliberate exaggeration.

- **Proposed topic:** Acids and bases / Acid-base titration apparatus
- **Difficulty:** Medium

**Question:** Which apparatus is most suitable for delivering a measured but variable volume of solution during an acid–base titration?

A. Burette  
B. Pipette  
C. Volumetric flask  
D. Measuring cylinder

**Correct option:** A

**Explanation:** A burette delivers accurately measured variable volumes, usually during titration. A pipette delivers a fixed volume, a volumetric flask is used to prepare a fixed volume, and a measuring cylinder is less precise.

**Internal content checks**

- **Answer logic:** A is the only defensible answer for delivering a measured variable volume during titration.
- **Concept distinction:** The held item tests the colour behaviour of an acid-base indicator. This draft tests the function of titration apparatus.
