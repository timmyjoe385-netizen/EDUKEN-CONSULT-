## Replacement question

Under Nigeria's 1999 Constitution, the Senate is made up of how many members?

A. 109  
B. 108  
C. 360  
D. 469

**Correct answer:** A

**Explanation:** Section 48 of Nigeria's 1999 Constitution provides for three senators from each state and one senator representing the Federal Capital Territory. With 36 states, this gives 108 state senators plus one FCT senator, making 109 members.
