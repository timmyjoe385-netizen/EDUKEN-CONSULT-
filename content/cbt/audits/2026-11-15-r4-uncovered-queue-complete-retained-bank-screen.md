# R4 Uncovered Queue — Complete Retained-Bank Collision Screen (15 November 2026)

## Scope and method

This report closes the first retained-bank collision-screen pass across all 26 targets that remained without documented replacement drafts in the 14 November checkpoint. Each held target was matched by exact held location and compared with the 1,439 provisionally retained R4 questions in the same subject. The comparison considered the question stem, options, topic and subtopic; conceptual overlap was assessed separately from wording.

A target was not given a new proposal where the retained bank already tests the same fact, definition, principle or learning outcome. These are draft-screening decisions only. They do not independently establish that a held row is a confirmed duplicate, and they are not qualified academic approval.

The 12 earlier findings are recorded in `2026-11-15-r4-uncovered-queue-retained-bank-collision-screen.md`. This addendum records the other 14 targets, completing the 26-target queue screen.
