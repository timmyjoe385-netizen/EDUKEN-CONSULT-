## Batch 31 — DUP-106 (Biology)
- Held location: batch 37, row 13.
- Topic: Human biology / Excretion.
- Held learning outcome: identify the glomerulus as the site of ultrafiltration.
- Decision: **Retain one provisional internal replacement draft for human academic review.**
- New draft:
  - **Question:** In a healthy human nephron, most glucose and amino acids are selectively reabsorbed into the blood in the
  - **A.** proximal convoluted tubule.
  - **B.** Bowman's capsule.
  - **C.** loop of Henle.
  - **D.** collecting duct.
  - **Key:** A
  - **Explanation:** The proximal convoluted tubule selectively reabsorbs nearly all filtered glucose and amino acids, together with much of the water and useful ions, into the blood. Bowman's capsule receives the filtrate; it is not the main site of selective glucose and amino-acid reabsorption.
  - **Difficulty:** medium

Other renal items cover filtrate entry into Bowman's capsule, ADH and collecting ducts, and the loop of Henle's role in concentrating urine; none asks this selective reabsorption question.
- Existing-proposal screen: all 18 Biology proposals reviewed. Highest lexical Dice score: **0.296**, against DUP-74 (pressure driving ultrafiltration); no close proposal-level match was identified.
