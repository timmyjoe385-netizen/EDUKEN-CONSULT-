## 5. Required sign-off fields — one record per exact draft version

For every proposal ID and every new R4 queue draft that is retained for review, capture:

1. Subject and reviewer name.
2. Exact proposal/draft ID **and** held source batch/row.
3. Exact question stem and four options reviewed (or immutable content hash).
4. Correct answer and explanation confirmed.
5. Current UTME syllabus fit confirmed, with topic/subtopic.
6. Duplicate/overlap check and any flagged comparison considered.
7. Decision: **APPROVED**, **APPROVED WITH REQUIRED EDITS**, **REJECTED**, or **HOLD — MORE REVIEW REQUIRED**.
8. Required edits or reason for rejection/hold.
9. Reviewer date and attributable evidence (signed worksheet or attributable written confirmation).

An “approved with required edits” item stays blocked until the edited exact version is reviewed again. An item without all required fields remains pending.
