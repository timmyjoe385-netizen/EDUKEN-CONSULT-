## Proposed replacement

**Question:** One major feature of the 1922 Clifford Constitution in Nigeria was the introduction of:

A. the elective principle in Lagos and Calabar  
B. universal adult suffrage throughout the country  
C. a presidential system of government  
D. regional self-government in all parts of Nigeria

**Correct answer:** A

**Explanation:** The Clifford Constitution of 1922 introduced the elective principle into the Nigerian Legislative Council for a limited number of seats representing Lagos and Calabar. It did not establish universal adult suffrage, a presidential system, or regional self-government throughout Nigeria.
