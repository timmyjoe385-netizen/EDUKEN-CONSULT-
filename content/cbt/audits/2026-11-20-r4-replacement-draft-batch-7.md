- **Proposed topic:** Elections / Election observation
- **Difficulty:** Medium

**Question:** A civil society organisation sends trained, non-partisan personnel to observe the opening of polling stations, voting and counting. What is the main purpose of this activity?

A. To provide an independent assessment of how the election is conducted  
B. To take over the duties of the electoral commission  
C. To determine which candidate should be declared the winner  
D. To settle legal disputes between candidates

**Correct option:** A

**Explanation:** Election observers monitor relevant stages of the electoral process and document their findings. Their reports can provide independent information about whether the process was conducted transparently and in accordance with applicable standards. Observers do not administer the election, declare the official result or determine election petitions.
