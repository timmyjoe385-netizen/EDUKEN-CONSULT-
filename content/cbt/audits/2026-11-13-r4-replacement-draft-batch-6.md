**Question:** During an experiment, the volume of gas produced by a reaction is measured at intervals. Which quantity gives the average rate of gas production over a chosen interval?

A. The total volume of gas measured at the end of the experiment  
B. The change in gas volume divided by the elapsed time  
C. The elapsed time divided by the change in gas volume  
D. The mass of catalyst divided by the initial gas volume

**Correct option:** B

**Explanation:** Average rate of gas production over a time interval is the change in gas volume divided by the time taken for that change. Its units are volume per unit time, such as cm³ s⁻¹.
