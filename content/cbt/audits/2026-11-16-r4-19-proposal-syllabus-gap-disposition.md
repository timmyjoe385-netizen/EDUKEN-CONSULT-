## Purpose and interpretation

This record narrows the meaning of the syllabus findings. The fact that an exact word was not found in a syllabus PDF text search does **not** prove that the concept is outside the syllabus. A concept may be included under a broader heading or objective, or appear in the detailed IBASS e-syllabus. Conversely, a broad heading does not automatically prove that every advanced sub-concept is suitable for UTME.

JAMB's official website links to IBASS as its syllabus system. The eligibility checker also lists the approved UTME subject options. The current public syllabus system should be checked directly by subject and topic before finalising a proposal. The reviewed sources do not establish a separate 2027 syllabus revision or an official JAMB approval of these draft questions.

The exact detailed objectives for each flagged item must be opened in JAMB IBASS, subject by subject. For every target, record:
1. The exact official topic heading and objective wording;
2. The relevant subject and syllabus location;
3. Whether the proposed question tests the objective directly or goes beyond it;
4. The decision: retain, revise or replace;
5. The complete question-level factual/key/distractor check.

If the IBASS page or syllabus does not expose enough detail to decide, leave the target unresolved rather than inferring coverage. Do not label an item "out of syllabus" based solely on a missing exact phrase.
