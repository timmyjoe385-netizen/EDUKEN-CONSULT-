## Draft 1 — Chemistry

- **Held target:** DUP-97 — batch 37 row 21
- **Held topic:** Chemical reactions / Acids and carbonates
- **Draft topic:** Chemical reactions / Identification of carbon dioxide
- **Stem:** When carbon dioxide is bubbled through limewater, the solution turns milky because:
- **Options:**
  - A. Insoluble calcium carbonate forms
  - B. Oxygen is released
  - C. Sodium chloride crystallises
  - D. The water evaporates
- **Answer:** A
- **Explanation:** Carbon dioxide reacts with calcium hydroxide in limewater to form insoluble calcium carbonate and water. The suspended calcium carbonate makes the solution appear milky.
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Highest same-subject bank score:** 0.408 (retained question on the observation when acidified silver nitrate is added to chloride ions)
- **Highest canonical proposal score:** 0.342 (DUP-216; temporary hardness in water)
- **Screen result:** Both below 0.42. This tests identification of carbon dioxide using limewater rather than the held acid-carbonate reaction.
- **Review note:** Reviewer should confirm the practical test, reaction explanation and syllabus relevance.

## Draft 2 — Biology

- **Held target:** DUP-134 — batch 35 row 13
- **Held topic:** Human physiology / Blood components
- **Draft topic:** Human physiology / Blood clotting
- **Stem:** Which plasma protein is converted into fibrin during the final stages of blood clotting?
- **Options:**
  - A. Fibrinogen
  - B. Haemoglobin
  - C. Albumin
  - D. Insulin
- **Answer:** A
- **Explanation:** During blood clotting, thrombin converts soluble fibrinogen in the plasma into insoluble fibrin strands. The fibrin forms a mesh that helps stabilise the blood clot.
- **Difficulty:** Medium
- **Highest same-subject bank score:** 0.405 (retained question about haemoglobin-containing red blood cells and oxygen transport)
- **Highest canonical proposal score:** 0.235 (DUP-70; central vacuole)
- **Screen result:** Both below 0.42. This tests the role of a plasma clotting protein rather than oxygen transport by red blood cells.
- **Review note:** Reviewer should verify the sequence and terminology of the clotting process.

## Draft 3 — Biology
