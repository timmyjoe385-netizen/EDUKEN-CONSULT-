## Batch 40 — DUP-179 (Chemistry)
- Held location: batch 39, row 21.
- Topic: Separation techniques / Chromatography.
- Held learning outcome: explain why a component that travels farther in paper chromatography is generally more soluble in the mobile phase.
- Decision: **Retain one provisional internal replacement draft for human academic review.**
- New draft:
  - **Question:** In a paper chromatogram, a dye spot moves 4.0 cm from the origin while the solvent front moves 8.0 cm. What is the Rf value of the dye?
  - **A.** 0.25.
  - **B.** 0.50.
  - **C.** 2.0.
  - **D.** 4.0.
  - **Key:** B
  - **Explanation:** The Rf value is the distance travelled by the substance divided by the distance travelled by the solvent front. Therefore, Rf = 4.0 ÷ 8.0 = 0.50.
  - **Difficulty:** medium
