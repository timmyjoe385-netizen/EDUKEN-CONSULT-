## Proposed replacement question

The appearance of droplets of liquid water at the margins of leaves, released through specialised pores, is known as:

A. Guttation  
B. Transpiration  
C. Translocation  
D. Condensation

**Proposed correct answer:** A

**Explanation:** Guttation is the loss of liquid water droplets from leaf margins or tips through specialised structures called hydathodes. It is commonly associated with root pressure when transpiration is low. Transpiration, by contrast, is the loss of water vapour, mainly through stomata.
