## What this crosswalk does and does not establish

1. “Explicit topic alignment” means the proposal's broad learning area is named in the official syllabus. It is not a claim that every detail, distractor or difficulty level is automatically suitable.
2. “Broad-area relation” is not clearance. The exact concept must be checked against the full objective and approved teaching level.
3. A term not found in the searchable PDF is not proof that JAMB excludes it; however, a broad heading is not proof that an advanced sub-concept is suitable.
4. No independent academic reviewer has approved any of the 19 proposals.
5. This crosswalk does not modify any proposal stem, key, source row or inventory entry.
