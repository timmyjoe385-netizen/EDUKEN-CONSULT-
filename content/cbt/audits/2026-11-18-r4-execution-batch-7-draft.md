## Proposed replacement

**Question:** In many dicotyledonous stems, secondary growth in thickness is mainly brought about by the activity of the:

A. vascular cambium  
B. apical meristem  
C. epidermis  
D. root cap

**Correct answer:** A

**Explanation:** The vascular cambium is a lateral meristem. During secondary growth, it produces secondary xylem towards the inside and secondary phloem towards the outside, increasing the thickness of the stem.
