- Decision: **Retain one provisional internal replacement draft for human academic review.**
- New draft:

  - **A.** is greater than its average total cost.
  - **B.** is lower than its average total cost.
  - **C.** is exactly equal to its average total cost.
  - **D.** is unrelated to its average total cost.
