### DUP-228 — Economics — Capital flight

**Proposed stem**

The movement of financial assets out of a country, often in response to economic or political uncertainty, is known as:

**Options**
- A. Capital flight
- B. Capital formation
- C. Capital deepening
- D. Capital consumption

**Proposed key:** A

**Explanation:** Capital flight is the movement of financial assets out of a country. It may occur when investors respond to economic or political uncertainty or expect better returns elsewhere.

**Why revise:** Replaces a declarative definition that effectively gives away the answer with a direct completion question and parallel economic terms.

### DUP-109 — Economics — Recession

**Proposed stem**

Which term describes a significant decline in overall economic activity, commonly associated with falling output and rising unemployment?

**Options**
- A. Recession
- B. Inflation
- C. Economic expansion
- D. Currency appreciation

**Proposed key:** A

**Explanation:** A recession is a significant downturn in overall economic activity. It is commonly associated with reduced production, income and employment, although the precise statistical definition can vary.

**Why revise:** Removes the original definition-like stem and avoids an option that simply repeats the definition.

### DUP-102 — Government — Commonwealth of Nations

**Proposed stem**

Which of the following best describes the Commonwealth of Nations?

**Options**
- A. A voluntary association of independent sovereign states that cooperate on shared interests
- B. A supranational government with authority to legislate for all member states
- C. A military alliance with a unified armed force
- D. A customs union with a common external tariff

**Proposed key:** A

**Explanation:** The Commonwealth is a voluntary association of independent and sovereign states. It promotes cooperation and shared values but is not a single government, military alliance or customs union.

**Why revise:** Turns the statement into a direct question while retaining the intended distinction.

### DUP-123 — Government — Political ideology

**Proposed stem**

What is meant by a political ideology?

**Options**
- A. A coherent set of political ideas and principles that guides views on government and society
- B. A record of the number of votes cast in an election
- C. A list of court judgments on constitutional disputes
- D. A schedule of government revenue and expenditure

**Proposed key:** A

**Explanation:** A political ideology is a connected set of ideas and values about political authority, society and the role of government. It provides a framework for interpreting political issues and proposing how society should be organised.

**Why revise:** Removes the answer-revealing declarative stem and makes the prompt direct.

### DUP-221 — Government — Caretaker government

**Proposed stem**

A temporary administration that manages routine government affairs while a new substantive government is being formed is called a:

**Options**
- A. Caretaker government
- B. Coalition government
- C. Federal government
- D. Military government

**Proposed key:** A

**Explanation:** A caretaker government temporarily manages routine affairs during a transition. It is generally expected to avoid major policy decisions that should be made by the incoming substantive government.

**Why revise:** Replaces the original answer-revealing definition with a completion prompt and distinct government forms.

### DUP-68 — Use of English — Homographs

**Proposed stem**

In the sentence “Strong winds damaged the roof”, which transcription best represents the pronunciation of “winds”?

**Options**
- A. /waɪndz/
- B. /wɪndz/
- C. /wɪnz/
- D. /waɪnz/

**Proposed key:** B

**Explanation:** In this sentence, “winds” is the plural noun referring to moving air and is pronounced /wɪndz/. The verb “wind”, meaning to turn or twist, has the vowel sound /aɪ/.

**Why revise:** Tests the pronunciation distinction directly and replaces overlapping semantic distractors. The English reviewer should confirm the phonetic transcription convention used in the bank.
