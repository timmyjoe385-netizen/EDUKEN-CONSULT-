## Draft 1 — Government

- **Held target:** DUP-125 — batch 29 row 33
- **Held topic:** Elections / Secret ballot
- **Draft topic:** Elections / Representative democracy
- **Stem:** The process by which citizens choose individuals to represent them in a legislature is known as:
- **Options:**
  - A. Election
  - B. Census
  - C. Referendum
  - D. Coup d'état
- **Answer:** A
- **Explanation:** An election is the process through which eligible voters choose candidates to occupy public offices, including seats in a legislature. A census counts a population, while a referendum asks voters to decide a specific public question.

## Draft 2 — Economics

- **Held target:** DUP-225 — batch 31 row 30
- **Held topic:** International trade / Import quotas
- **Draft topic:** International trade / Import tariffs
- **Stem:** A tax imposed on goods brought into a country is known as:
- **Options:**
  - A. Import tariff
  - B. Export subsidy
  - C. Income tax
  - D. Excise refund
- **Answer:** A
- **Explanation:** An import tariff is a tax imposed on imported goods. It can raise the domestic price of the imported product and generate government revenue.
- **Difficulty:** Medium
- **Highest same-subject bank similarity:** 0.329 (a retained question about taxes on goods and services at the point of purchase)
- **Highest canonical proposal similarity:** 0.262 (DUP-25; concealment of taxable income)
- **Highest prior supplemental-draft similarity:** 0.226
- **Screen result:** All three maxima are below 0.42.
- **Review note:** Reviewer should verify the distinction between an import tariff, an import quota and other forms of taxation, and confirm syllabus alignment.
