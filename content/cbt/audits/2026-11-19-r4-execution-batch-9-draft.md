## Replacement question

A population census that records each person at the place where they are physically present on the designated census night is known as a:

A. De facto census  
B. De jure census  
C. Sample survey  
D. Vital registration

**Correct answer:** A

**Explanation:** A de facto census counts people where they are physically present on the specified census date or night. A de jure census, by contrast, records people according to their usual or legally recognised place of residence.
